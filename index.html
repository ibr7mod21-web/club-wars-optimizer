How to use this tool

Quick start

  1. Pick your cards. Open Disaster card settings (or run the quick setup), tick the cards you have unlocked, and enter the war points each one shows in game. The plan only uses ticked cards.
  2. Enter your depot. Type your war items or use the plus and minus buttons. The plan updates as you type.
  3. Read the plan. It is sorted by points per energy, so if you might get interrupted, fire from the top down.
  4. Press Fire after each attack you cast in the game. The items come out of your depot and the plan recalculates.

What else it shows

  • Fire next: the cast that costs your total plan the least, if you need to attack before you are ready.
  • Spare items: what is left after the best plan, so giving it away costs you nothing.
  • Blocked cards: items at zero that stop cards from being cast.
  • Items to chase: which items would add the most points if you got more of them.
  • Burst planner: the best attacks to fit inside one energy bank. Set your Jackpot boosters under Battle boosters to line up the biggest hits for the Jackpot window.

Good to know

  • Points depend on card level. When a card levels up, update its points in Disaster card settings. The plan is only as accurate as those numbers.
  • Your data stays on your device. Items and settings are saved in this browser only. Your phone and laptop will not share them, and clearing browser data erases them.
  • A card is missing or wrong? Edit its recipe, damage or energy, or add a new disaster, in Disaster card settings.
