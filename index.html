How to use this tool

Quick start

  1. Pick your cards. Open Disaster card settings (or the quick card setup), tick the cards you have unlocked, and enter the war points each one shows in-game. The plan only uses ticked cards.
  2. Enter your depot. Type your war items or use + and −. The plan updates as you type.
  3. Read the plan. Optimal Attack Plan is the best use of everything you have, sorted by points per energy. If you might get interrupted, fire from the top down.
  4. Hit "Fire ×1" after each attack you cast in the game. The items are taken out of your depot and the plan recalculates.

The extras

  • Safest attack to fire right now: the cast that costs your total plan the least, if you need to attack before you're ready.
  • Safe to give teammates: items left over after the best plan, so giving them away costs you nothing.
  • Bottleneck items: items stuck at zero that are blocking cards.
  • Best items to chase: which items would add the most points if you got more of them.
  • Energy-bank burst planner: the best attacks to fit inside one energy bank. Enter your bank size, and set your Jackpot boosters under Battle boosters to line up the biggest hits for the Jackpot window.

Good to know

  • Points depend on card level. When a card levels up, update its points in Disaster card settings. The plan is only as accurate as those numbers.
  • Your data stays on your device. Items and settings are saved in this browser only. Your phone and laptop won't share them, and clearing browser data wipes them.
  • Cards missing or wrong? You can edit recipes, damage and energy, or add a new disaster, at the bottom of Disaster card settings.
