How to use this tool

Quick start

  1. Pick your cards. Open Disaster card settings (or run the quick setup), tick every card you have unlocked, and type in the war points each one shows in game. The plan only ever uses ticked cards.
  2. Enter your war items. Type a number next to any item, or use the minus and plus buttons. The plan recalculates on every change.
  3. Read the plan. Attacks are numbered in firing order, best points per energy first. If you might get interrupted mid-war, fire from number 1 down and you lose the least.
  4. Press Fire after each cast in the game. That attack's items come out of your depot and the plan updates. Tapped the wrong one? Press Undo on the message that appears.

Reading the screen

  • Totals bar: war points, energy, damage and attacks for the whole plan. It stays pinned while you scroll.
  • Energy bar: one segment per cast, left to right in firing order. The white marks show where each energy bank runs out, so you can see how many refills the plan needs.
  • War items: the gold line under each item shows how much of it the plan uses. "All used" means every one is needed, "2 spare" means two are left over, and a red note means the item is at zero and locking cards.
  • Fire next: the bar at the bottom shows the safest attack to fire right now. It always matches attack number 1.

Planning ahead

  • Spare items: left over after the best plan, so giving them to clubmates costs you nothing.
  • Blocked: items at zero that stop cards from being cast at all.
  • Items to chase: which items would add the most points if you got more. The full bar is the gain from three more, the darker part is the gain from just one. Use it when asking your club for items.
  • Energy bank and Jackpot: the best opener that fits inside one energy bank, at no cost to the rest of your plan. Enter how many Jackpot boosters you have and each one gets its own best window, since Jackpot doubles the war points of every hit while it is active.

Good to know

  • Points depend on card level. A card earns about 10% more war points per level. When one levels up, update it in Disaster card settings. The plan is only as accurate as those numbers.
  • Your data stays on your device. Items and settings are saved in this browser only. Your phone and laptop keep separate copies, and clearing browser data erases them.
  • Put it on your home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app.
  • A card is missing or wrong? Edit its recipe, damage or energy, or add a new disaster, in Disaster card settings.
